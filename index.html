About this assessment & methodology

This assessment reflects Merkle's analytics consulting practice — a structured, vendor-neutral way to match analytics needs to the platforms in market, refined across enterprise platform selections. It is not a feature checklist: every judgement in the underlying rubric encodes our consultants' hands-on experience of where each platform genuinely performs or falls short in production.

The method is deliberately simple and defensible. Capabilities are grouped into pillars. You assign a MoSCoW priority to each capability — Must, Should, Could or Won't — to express how important it is. Each platform's coverage of a capability is measured from a yes/no assessment of its sub-capabilities, maintained in our master rubric, and rolls up to a priority-weighted fit score (Must 3, Should 2, Could 1). A Must a platform cannot meet at all is treated as a disqualifier rather than a deduction, so a strong all-rounder never outranks a platform that clears every non-negotiable. The output is a best-of-breed recommendation: a lead platform, plus any specialist platforms that close priority gaps.

Assessments represent Merkle's point of view and are validated against current vendor releases at the time of engagement.
